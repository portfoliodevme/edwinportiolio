Sentinel Awareness

Consent-based social engineering awareness simulation platform

Project overview

Explanatory

Sentinel Awareness is a standalone, consent-based platform for simulating social engineering attacks in a controlled educational environment. Participants receive fictional simulated messages containing realistic lures and are guided to follow links to educational landing pages.

The platform never collects real passwords, PINs, one-time codes, tokens, cookies, or keystrokes. All behavioural events are recorded as SHA-256 digests, deduplicated per participant/scenario, and used only for aggregated analytics and reporting.

Admins can create campaigns, assign participants, publish training modules, deliver simulated messages, and export reports in PDF and Excel formats.

Objectives

Explanatory

Safe simulation

  • Fictional senders only
  • Links to educational pages
  • SHA-256 token digests
  • Automatic token expiry

Consent-based

  • Informed consent on registration
  • Withdrawal revokes tokens
  • No penalty required
  • Re-consent restores access

Aggregated analytics

  • Unique participant counts
  • Rates with zero-denominator guards
  • Pseudonymous exports
  • All numbers from DB

Role-based access

  • Admin manages campaigns/participants
  • Participant dashboard/inbox
  • Strict endpoint separation
  • CSRF on state-changing forms
